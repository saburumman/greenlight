Privacy Policy — Greenlight

Last updated: September 2026

Greenlight is an internal release-readiness tracking tool. It uses "Sign in with Atlassian" (OAuth 2.0) so that each user accesses Jira under their own Atlassian account and permissions — Greenlight has no shared or global Jira login of its own.

What information we collect

When you sign in with your Atlassian account, Greenlight receives and stores:

  • Your Atlassian account ID (a stable identifier, not your email)
  • Your name, email address, and avatar image, as provided by your Jira profile — used only to display who you are inside the app
  • An OAuth access token and refresh token, used solely to make Jira API requests on your behalf

How this information is used

This data is used only to operate Greenlight for your organization: to identify you within the app, and to read Jira issue data on your behalf when you use Greenlight's features. It is never sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

How long we keep it

Your OAuth tokens are stored only for as long as your Greenlight session is active. Signing out of Greenlight deletes your stored tokens immediately.

Who can access it

This data is accessible only to Greenlight's server-side infrastructure, operated by your organization for internal use. It is not exposed through any public API.
